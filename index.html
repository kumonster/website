10 GOLDEN FITNESS RULES

  1. Eat a balanced diet.
  2. Consume enough protein.
  3. Drink water regularly.
  4. Exercise consistently.
  5. Prioritize proper technique.
  6. Get 7–9 hours of sleep.
  7. Allow your muscles to recover.
  8. Eat fruits and vegetables.
  9. Increase training difficulty gradually.
  10. Be patient and stay consistent.
